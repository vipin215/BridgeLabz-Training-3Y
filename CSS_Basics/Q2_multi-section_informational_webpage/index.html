Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colorful markets and rich heritage.

History

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is India's first planned city. Its architecture combines Rajput and Mughal styles.

Famous Places

Visit Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

Traditional Food

Jaipur is well known for Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, Pyaaz Kachori and delicious Rajasthani Thali.

Culture & Festivals

The city celebrates Teej, Gangaur and Jaipur Literature Festival with great enthusiasm.
